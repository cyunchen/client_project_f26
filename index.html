Use this site to prepare for your engaged learning experience, manage your project, and reflect on what you learned.

Why Use This Resource?

Real-world projects rarely follow a perfect plan. This site helps you find the guidance you need at each stage, so you can spend less time searching for materials and more time learning and moving your project forward.

  • Follow a clear process: Know what to do at each stage.
  • Find practical support: Find templates, checklists, and guides.
  • Build professional skills: Practice communication, teamwork, and project management.
  • Turn experience into evidence: Use your experience in resumes, portfolios, and interviews.
