Navigating Your Active Project

Use these resources to manage your project, gather feedback, and adapt as your project develops.

Phase 3: During Your Experience

Conduct research, track progress, and maintain clear communication with project sponsors.

What to Do During Your Project

  • Conduct fieldwork and research: Apply human-centered methods to observe contexts and identify insights.
  • Keep your partner updated: Share progress, challenges, and changes regularly.
  • Use feedback: Gather, evaluate, and incorporate feedback as the project develops.
